Link Them All: privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Link Them All is a free puzzle game for Android by mawistudios. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no analytics. This page explains what happens to your data when you play it.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your progress, your settings and a few play statistics (levels solved and solved cleanly, hints left, links, collisions, taps and time played) on your phone. The game does not send this data anywhere.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy this saved progress into your phone's backup in your Google account, so that it can be restored on a new phone. Google handles that backup under the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play

You install the game from Google Play, which handles downloads and updates under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives the developer only statistics without names, such as the number of installs and crash reports.

What the game does not do

The game shows no ads, sells nothing, contains no analytics, tracking or advertising ID, has no sign-in or account and does not collect, sell or share personal data. It sends nothing over the internet. It asks for two Android permissions: internet access, which the app framework it is built on declares and which the game does not use, and vibration, used for haptic feedback.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

RESET in the game's settings deletes your progress and statistics on the phone, and uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved.
